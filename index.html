Welcome

The Indie Film Collective is a creative community for independent filmmakers, storytellers and visual artists who are making work without a studio behind them. We run monthly screenings, hands-on workshops, and a matchmaking board for people who need a collaborator. Membership is free, and most of what we do happens in person.
