Film Screenings

Every screening we run is built around films that struggled to find a room. Submissions come from first-time directors, students and people making work between day jobs, and the selection committee watches everything that arrives. Nothing is programmed because of who made it.

Screenings run on the second Thursday of each month and last about two hours, including a director Q&A. Doors open half an hour early, seating is unreserved, and there is no charge, though we pass a bucket for the venue.

What to expect on the night

  • Three to five short films, or one feature, depending on the programme
  • A Q&A with at least one director from the night's selection
  • A short break between the films and the discussion
  • An open floor afterwards, where filmmakers looking for crew can introduce themselves

If you have a film you would like considered, bring it up at the open floor or post it on the collaboration board.
