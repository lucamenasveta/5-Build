Collaborations

Most independent projects die for want of one person: a sound recordist, a colourist, someone who owns a van and has a free weekend. The collaboration board exists to close that gap. Post what you are making and what you are missing, and we circulate it to the membership.

Posts stay on the board for sixty days and can be renewed. We ask that you say up front whether the work is paid, deferred or unpaid, because that is the question everybody asks second and it saves everyone's time to answer it first.

Roles most often requested

  • Sound recordists, by a wide margin
  • Colourists who can work from log footage
  • Producers willing to take on scheduling and locations
  • Actors for unpaid short films with a credit and a copy
  • Anyone with a vehicle and a free weekend

New to the collective? The easiest way in is to come to a screening or sit in on a workshop first.
