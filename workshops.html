Workshops

Our workshops exist because most filmmaking knowledge is still passed hand to hand. Sessions are small, capped at twelve people, and taught by people who are actively working rather than by full-time instructors. You bring your own laptop and, where relevant, your own footage.

Each workshop runs three hours on a Saturday morning and costs nothing to attend, but places go quickly and no-shows lose priority for the next session. Bring a drive with at least 50GB free if you are coming to an editing workshop.

The current series, in order

  1. Editing rhythm: cutting a scene four different ways and watching what changes
  2. Available-light cinematography: shooting a usable night exterior with one light and a reflector
  3. Sound on a budget: getting clean dialogue with a boom, a recorder and a quiet room
  4. Story structure: breaking a 90-page script down to its load-bearing scenes
  5. Colour grading: matching shots from two cameras without making either look broken

Workshop places are announced at the monthly screenings before they go out to the wider membership.
